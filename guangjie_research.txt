# Research on "Guangjie" (逛街) in Stock Market Context

## 1. Meaning of "Guangjie" (逛街)

In the context of the stock market, "逛街" (Guangjie), which literally means "shopping" or "strolling the street", can refer to a few things:

### A. Specific Company: MOGU Inc. (Mogujie / 蘑菇街)
The most likely specific company reference is **MOGU Inc.** (Chinese: 蘑菇街, Pinyin: Mogu Jie).
- The term "Guangjie" is part of its Chinese name and describes its core business (browsing/shopping for fashion).
- Users often shorten names or use related terms as nicknames.

### B. Market Slang
In Chinese trading slang, "逛街" (Guangjie) or "大盘逛街" (Market strolling) often refers to a **sideways market** (market moving in a narrow range with low volatility), implying the price is just "wandering around" without direction.

### C. Sector Reference
It can loosely refer to the **Retail** or **Consumption** sector, specifically offline retail (department stores, shopping streets) vs online.
